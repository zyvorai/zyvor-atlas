# Atlas: enterprise subscription

Atlas is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Professional | Entry edition, updates and upgrades | Business hours, next-business-day critical response |
| Enterprise | Advanced edition, updates and upgrades | Priority business hours, four-business-hour critical response |
| Premium | Contracted enterprise scope and critical escalation | 24x7 critical intake, one-hour initial response target |

## List rates

Priced per usable TB per year, billed annually in advance, with an annual minimum per plan. List rates are in US dollars excl. tax worldwide, with the INR equivalent (USD x 99.12, incl. 18% GST) shown for India. The final quote depends on scope, term and volume, and is issued by Zyvor.

| Plan | Per usable TB per year (USD, excl. tax) | Annual minimum (USD) | Per usable TB per year (India, INR incl. 18% GST) | Annual minimum (India, INR incl. GST) |
|------|------------------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Professional | $12 | $6,000 | INR 1,189 | INR 594,720 |
| Enterprise | $22 | $12,000 | INR 2,181 | INR 1,189,440 |
| Premium | $34 | $24,000 | INR 3,370 | INR 2,378,880 |

**Volume.** The rate for the whole subscription drops with total usable capacity: over 500 TB, 10% off; over 2 PB, 20% off; over 10 PB, 30% off (or a flat per-node price, on request). A three-year prepay takes a further 10% off. Volume and multi-year discounts together never exceed 40%.

**Edge.** A single-pod edge site (the [edge profile](NATIVE_EDGE.md)) is a flat $1,500 per site per year (INR 148,680 incl. GST in India), so a fleet of small sites is not priced by capacity it barely has.

**Examples** (Enterprise, $22 per usable TB, annual minimum $12,000 applied after discounts):

| Usable capacity | Calculation | Per year |
|-----------------|-------------|---------:|
| 400 TB | $22 x 400 = $8,800, below the minimum | $12,000 |
| 1 PB (1,000 TB) | $22 x 1,000 x 0.9 = $19,800 | $19,800 |
| 2 PB | $22 x 2,000 x 0.9 = $39,600 | $39,600 |
| 3 PB | $22 x 3,000 x 0.8 = $52,800 | $52,800 |

The minimum is the price floor, so the volume discount only lowers the bill once the discounted amount is above it (about 545 TB on Enterprise, 500 TB on Professional and 706 TB on Premium, before discount).

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums are annual contract values and apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Rights under the Zyvor Production License v1.0 continue on the terms of the licence.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
