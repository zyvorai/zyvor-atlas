# Atlas: enterprise subscription

Atlas is available under an annual **enterprise subscription** for production use. One subscription covers product access, updates and upgrades, and the stated support level. There is no separate support surcharge.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Community | Evaluation and non-production use, where the licence allows it | Community resources |
| Professional | Entry edition, updates and upgrades | Business hours, next-business-day critical response |
| Enterprise | Advanced edition, updates and upgrades | Priority business hours, four-business-hour critical response |
| Premium | Contracted enterprise scope and critical escalation | 24x7 critical intake, one-hour initial response target |

## List rates

Priced per usable TB per year, billed annually in advance, with an annual minimum per plan. These are list rates in USD; the final quote depends on scope, term and volume, and is issued by Zyvor.

| Plan | Per usable TB per year | Annual minimum |
|------|------------------------|----------------|
| Professional | $12 | $12,000 |
| Enterprise | $22 | $24,000 |
| Premium | $34 | $48,000 |

**Volume.** The rate for the whole subscription drops with total usable capacity: over 500 TB, 20% off; over 2 PB, 35% off; over 10 PB, 50% off (or a flat per-node price, on request). A three-year prepay takes a further 10% off.

**Edge.** A single-pod edge site (the [edge profile](NATIVE_EDGE.md)) is a flat $1,500 per site per year, so a fleet of small sites is not priced by capacity it barely has.

**Example.** 1 PB usable on Enterprise: $22 x 1,000 TB x 0.8 = $17,600 per year.

Atlas itself is Apache-2.0 and free to run ([licensing](LICENSING.md)); the subscription buys supported releases, the stated response targets and a named technical contact. Hardware is not included: Atlas runs on your servers. Rates are quoted and invoiced by Zyvor: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev).

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Open-source rights, where they apply, continue under the licence.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
